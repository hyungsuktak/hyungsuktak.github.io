I am Hyungsuk Tak, an associate professor in the Department of Statistics, Department of Astronomy and Astrophysics, and the Institute for Computational and Data Science at Pennsylvania State University.

My research develops statistical models and computational methods for complex data, with a focus on astronomical time series and Bayesian computation. In astrostatistics, I study dependence in multiband active galactic nucleus (AGN) variability, using structured multivariate continuous-time autoregressive moving-average (MCARMA) models to characterize how brightness varies within and across optical bands. My methodological research also examines Markov chain Monte Carlo methods, including repelling-attracting Hamiltonian Monte Carlo (RA-HMC), for sampling high-dimensional, multimodal distributions.
